# Footage

Links to videos about techniques, systems and styles. Video is never committed to this repo.

One `## Subject` heading per technique, system or style, then one row per clip under it, in these columns:

| Clip | Link | Timestamp | Version | Claims to show | Watched by Mustafa |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Claims to show: what the title, description or transcript says the clip shows.
- Watched by Mustafa: `no` until he has watched the clip, then `yes` and what he confirmed.
