# Dissection axes

Version 1. Approved by Mustafa on 2026-11-13.

Every dissection doc fills these axes, in this order, under these names. An axis that cannot be filled is marked "Not observable", with the shot or source that would resolve it.

Review: this list is reviewed after five characters have been dissected.

Changes: propose the change, wait for Mustafa's approval, then raise the version and add a row to the history. Never change the list silently.

## Axes

1. Silhouette and proportions
2. Head and face construction
3. Eyes
4. Hair: shape language, clumps, highlight, shadow
5. Skin: tone, shading bands, rim light
6. Outlines and inner lines: where outlines appear, thickness, colour, where they vanish; which lines are drawn onto the surface instead
7. Clothing: structure, layering, trim, how pieces end
8. Detail and ornament: where detail clusters, where the surface is left plain, asymmetry, emblems and gems
9. Materials: how cloth, metal, leather, glass and gems respond to light
10. Colour: dominant, secondary, accent; saturation; where black and white are used; for each main colour, its lit tone and its shadow tone
11. Weapon: how it follows or breaks the character's palette and shapes
12. Readability: what identifies the character at a distance and in motion
13. Fit with the world: how the character reads against its region's palette
